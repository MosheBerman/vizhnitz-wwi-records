DatePlaceEventWhoEffect on Jews / recordsSrc

Reading rule for this whole site: loss is not destruction. A missing or re-created register proves loss. “Destroyed” needs its own source. A source that says “destroyed” with no fire, unit, date, eyewitness, or inventory is shown here as an assertion, honestly made, not as proof. Hypothetical routes and mechanisms are dashed or hollow, never solid.
Disproof check, applied to every fire: before a fire is allowed to explain a missing register, ask what survived it. Pre-1918 Jewish registers from this same fond are on FamilySearch today, Kotzman births 1902–1907 among them (Fond 1245-1/941), and Czernowitz, Sadagora, and Wiznitz's own Christian books all came through the same war. A fire that “destroyed the records” has to explain the survivors, or it explains nothing.
